# Reel music

Drop royalty-free tracks you have the rights to use here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.wav`),
e.g. from the Pixabay Music or YouTube Audio Library. Daily reels rotate through them.

Do not add film or chart songs: Instagram detects copyrighted music and mutes or blocks the reel.
With no files here, reels are posted with a silent audio track.
