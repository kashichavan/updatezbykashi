    Before you hit send

    A short, honest outreach playbook

    01

    Verify the source first

    Open the source link. If the address belongs to a team inbox or an old page, say so in your note and ask for the right owner.

    02

    One role, one proof point

    Name the job, give one verifiable project or result, and ask one question. Under 120 words beats a full cover letter.

    03

    Follow up once

    Wait three working days. Send one short follow-up, then stop. Persistence is good; pressure is not.

    Why can't this page just scrape LinkedIn?

    LinkedIn profiles sit behind an auth wall, and scraping them violates the platform's terms. Instead, this studio gives you X-Ray search links you can open yourself, then checks whether that person has independently published a contact address in a job post, GitHub metadata, or the open web. When nothing is public, the honest answer is “not found” — not a made-up address that bounces.
