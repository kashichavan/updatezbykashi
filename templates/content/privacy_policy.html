Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026 · Effective Date: August 19, 2026

At Kashii Updatez (accessible at kashiiupdatez.online), your privacy is one of our utmost priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Kashii Updatez and how we use, safeguard, and disclose it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at kashiiupdatez@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or interact with Kashii Updatez, we may collect information in several ways:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you contact us via our Contact Form, send an email, or submit application inquiries, we may collect your name, email address, message contents, and any attachments.
  • Automated Log Files: Like standard web platforms, Kashii Updatez utilizes log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Interactive Tool Data: Code snippets executed in the Visual Code Debugger are processed temporarily in memory to generate runtime Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) traces and are never sold or shared with external parties.

Resume tools

Resume text you upload, paste or type is sent to our server and may be sent to our AI providers, Groq and NVIDIA, to score or rewrite it. Resume Studio saves the resumes it generates so you can reopen them. The Resume Builder keeps your draft in your own browser.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Kashii Updatez uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of our third-party vendors. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to kashiiupdatez.online and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners, Monetag & Push Notifications

Our advertising partners (including Google AdSense and Monetag) may use cookies, web beacons, and browser service workers on our site to deliver contextual ads and voluntary web push notifications. Each advertising partner has its own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data.

Users may opt out of web push notifications at any time directly through their web browser notification settings.

Kashii Updatez provides direct links to external employer career portals, Google Forms, official recruitment websites, YouTube tutorials (@pythonkashi), and Instagram (@ikashii_07). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party sites.

5. CCPA & GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request restriction of the processing of your personal data.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. Kashii Updatez does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

7. Consent & Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update our Privacy Policy periodically; changes are effective immediately upon posting.
