    What a strong bullet looks like

    Recruiters and ATS scanners look for results, not a list of duties. This is the Google XYZ formula.

    Weak example

    "Managed backend code and assisted with features. Helped optimize database queries and launches."

    No strong verb, no measured result, vague technologies.

    Strong example

    "Built an asynchronous task queue with Redis and Celery, cutting report generation from 40 seconds to 6 seconds."

    Active verb, a result you measured, core stack. Use your own numbers only.
