    Why not just read LinkedIn?

    LinkedIn profiles sit behind a login wall, and scraping them breaks LinkedIn's terms. So this page gives you X-Ray search links to open yourself, then checks whether the person has published a contact address in a job post, on GitHub or on the open web. When nothing is public, the honest answer is "not found", not a made-up address that bounces.
